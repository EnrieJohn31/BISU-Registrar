Requirements checklist

What we need from the offices before the next build steps · BISU Registrar, Balilihan Campus · Version 1, 17 October 2026

Why we need this

  • What is built so far works on made-up data. To go further we need the offices' real rules and documents.
  • Without them we would be guessing fees, requirements and ID formats. A guess here means wrong charges and wrong documents.
  • Every item names what it unlocks, so we know what to chase first.

Where we are

  • Built (fake data): staff accounts and roles, audit trail, client records.
  • Waiting on this list: services and fees, requests, queues, Registrar and Cashier work screens, documents, release, reports, supplier list.
  • Next build: services and fees (Part 05), then imports of the student/alumni list (Part 04, slice 2).
Ground rules for every request
  • Never ask for or send real student or alumni records, payment details or IDs. Ask for blank forms and made-up examples only.
  • Ask for the official, approved version of each document, not someone's memory of it.
  • For every answer write down who said it (name and position), the date, and the document name and page. Give it to the project lead, who records it in the decision register.
  • Photos or scans are fine if they are clear and contain no personal data.
